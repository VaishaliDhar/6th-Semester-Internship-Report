# 6th-Semester-Internship-Report
# Summer Internship — Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL)

## Civil Engineering | Pre-Construction & Project Planning

This repository documents my **45-day summer internship at Power Grid Corporation of India Limited (PGCIL), Srikona**, where I gained practical exposure to **pre-construction planning, quantity estimation, technical documentation, BOQ preparation, reinforcement scheduling, and project-related documentation** for upcoming civil works.

The internship provided an opportunity to apply academic knowledge to real infrastructure projects and understand the workflow involved in taking a civil work from **drawings and technical requirements to estimation, costing, tender-stage documentation, and project planning**.

## Key Areas of Work

### 📐 Quantity Estimation

Worked on drawing-based quantity estimation for civil and structural works by studying architectural and structural drawings and preparing detailed quantity take-offs for individual construction components.

The estimation workflow involved understanding:

* Structural geometry and dimensions
* Levels and elevations
* Concrete quantities
* Reinforcement quantities
* Masonry and finishing works
* Other applicable civil items

The quantities were cross-checked against the relevant drawings before being incorporated into project documentation.

---

### 📋 Bill of Quantities (BOQ)

Prepared and worked with **Bills of Quantities** based on measured quantities derived from project drawings.

The BOQ process included:

* Identifying applicable construction items
* Measuring and calculating quantities
* Applying relevant **CPWD DSR 2023** item codes and rates
* Considering applicable cost indices
* Referring to **PGCIL Letters of Award (LOAs)** and market rates for applicable non-schedule items
* Cross-checking quantities and documentation

---

### 🏗️ Bar Bending Schedule (BBS)

Prepared reinforcement schedules based on structural drawings and reinforcement details.

The work included:

* Member-wise reinforcement scheduling
* Bar diameter-wise quantity calculation
* Cutting-length calculations
* Accounting for bends and hooks
* Considering development length
* Calculating total reinforcement weight

The report documents reinforcement scheduling for structural elements such as **footings, walls, columns, beams, slabs, and staircases**.

## Projects Covered

The internship involved technical and estimation work associated with four upcoming civil works:

### 1. RCC Protective Bunker

An underground RCC protective bunker developed as part of disaster preparedness at the PGCIL facility.

Work involved exposure to:

* RCC structural components
* Raft foundation
* Walls and columns
* Staircase and mumty
* Reinforcement detailing
* Quantity estimation
* BBS and BOQ preparation

### 2. RCC Accessibility Ramp

An RCC ramp developed to provide barrier-free access at the Transit Camp and Control Room.

The project involved drawing interpretation, structural specifications, quantity estimation, BBS and BOQ preparation.

### 3. Bamboo Crib Wall

A slope and erosion-control structure proposed around vulnerable transmission tower locations.

This provided exposure to the use of sustainable, locally sourced materials for slope stabilisation and erosion protection.

### 4. Crane Shed

A civil and structural project involving a crane shed with **steel and RCC components**, providing exposure to composite structural systems and associated project documentation.

## 🏢 Pre-Construction & Project Planning Exposure

A significant part of the internship involved understanding how civil works progress through the **pre-award and post-award stages**.

The workflow included exposure to:

**Cost Estimate → Technical & Administrative Approval → Tendering → Work Order / LOA → Document Review → Material & Quality Control → Site Supervision → Billing → Contract Closing**

The LOA and work-order documentation included aspects such as:

* Work completion period
* Quantity variation
* Liquidated damages
* Defect liability
* Insurance
* Payment terms
* Payment tracking
* Dispute settlement and arbitration

This provided practical insight into how **technical estimation and documentation contribute to pre-award planning and project execution**.
